our little story

A small business,
made by hand

Navo Creation began with a simple love for beads, colour and quiet afternoons of making. Every bracelet, necklace and ring is threaded bead by bead — no factories, no machines, just careful hands and a lot of heart.

Because each piece is handmade, no two are ever quite the same. When you order from us, you're not just buying jewellery — you're supporting a small local maker and taking home something truly one of a kind.
